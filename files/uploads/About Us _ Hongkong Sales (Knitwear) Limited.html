OUR BUSINESS

Established over 60 years in fashion-knit industry, HKS takes pride in our inspirational expertise on yarn trends, product innovation and development, advanced knitting technologies, and diverse manufacturing capabilities.  Our commitment to consistent product quality and strategic market planning have positioned us in today’s global success.  

 

We earn our reputation in the industry from experience, pragmatism and pioneering spirit, values that continue to guide our future encounters and global expansion.
